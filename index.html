Red Cedar

Red Cedar is a quantum machine learning framework from Cascade Quantum. You train models on quantum simulators and quantum computers without having to master the low-level details of quantum technology. You bring a table of data; Red Cedar builds, trains and runs the quantum circuits.

The redcedar Python package (the SDK) is how you use Red Cedar from notebooks, scripts and the command line. It talks to a Red Cedar server, which runs the circuits, so nothing quantum needs to be installed on your machine.
